<p class="home-kicker">What it is</p>
<h2 id="about-intro-heading">A Shared Cluster for Teaching and Learning with Data</h2>
<p class="hub-lede">PARASOL is the computing platform behind Datahub. Students open Jupyter or RStudio in a browser, and the session runs in a container on a cluster of CPUs and GPUs that UC San Diego operates on campus.</p>
<p>Courses use it from introductory data science to graduate machine learning, and in biology, music, the social sciences, and public health. The same cluster runs research work outside a course, on the same terms as instruction.</p>
<p>PARASOL stands for Platform for Applied Research, Analytics, Science and Open Learning. It is the platform formerly called DSMLP, the Data Science and Machine Learning Platform. It also takes in the research work formerly called the Research Cluster.</p>

<p class="home-kicker">Who runs it</p>
<h2 id="about-who-runs-heading">Operations and Support</h2>
<p>UC San Diego IT Services builds and runs PARASOL, including Datahub, the cluster, the reservation app, and support at <a href="mailto:parasol@ucsd.edu">parasol@ucsd.edu</a>. IT Services also gives instructors guidance and training on setting up a course. Research IT supports research work on PARASOL at rcd-support@ucsd.edu, and helps researchers decide whether PARASOL or another campus platform fits their work.</p>
